Materials

Wood, vinyl
or composite.

Three ways to build the same project, at three different price points. We'll walk the yard with you and be honest about which one actually makes sense — not which one costs the most.

Wood
Cedar and pressure-treated. Warmest look, best price up front, wants a stain every few years.
Vinyl
Never needs painting, wipes clean, holds its color. A favorite for railings and front-yard fence.
Composite
Wood grain without the splinters or the sanding. Our most-requested decking, built to shrug off wet Northwest winters.
